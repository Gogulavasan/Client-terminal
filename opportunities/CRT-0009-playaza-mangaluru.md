# CRT-0009 · Playaza Entertainments (Malabar Group) · Mangaluru · score 86 (Hot)

**Why now:** Playaza signed a letter of intent with Nexus Malls for a 22,000 sq ft FEC in Mangaluru at Fiza by Nexus, with about INR 18 cr of investment. It is the company's first centre outside Kerala. Playaza also plans 6 centres in 18 months, covering Mangaluru, Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hyderabad, Coimbatore and Chennai. The planned Mangaluru mix is 9 indoor rides, 80+ arcade games, an ND cinema, soft play and bowling.
- https://www.amusementsandattractions.com/amusements/news/indian-family-entertainment-brand-set-to-develop-flagship-centre
- https://in.linkedin.com/company/playaza (company post says the Mangalore centre is "coming soon")

**Venue snapshot:** FEC operator expanding across South India. The host mall, Fiza by Nexus, has 0.7 msf of leasable area (https://www.nexusselecttrust.com/nexus-fiza). Playaza's existing sites include Centre Square Mall in Kochi (~20,000 sq ft) and the Mall of Travancore.

**Best-fit products**
- Mixed Reality: headset-free group play that fills a gap their rides and arcade don't cover.
- Car Simulation: full-motion rigs that draw repeat visits and support leagues.
- Escape Room: long dwell time and a high ticket price for teens and corporate groups.
- 360° Dome: a ticketed show anchor for school groups at the larger sites.

**Suggested angle:** Playaza is designing six new centres at once. Offer attractions they own outright, installed and serviced from Bengaluru, which sits close to their Karnataka and Tamil Nadu sites. A phased approach could start with Mixed Reality and Car Sim in Mangaluru, then roll out to the other centres.
